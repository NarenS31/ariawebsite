## Official references

- Khanmigo: https://www.khanmigo.ai/
  Audience-specific routes for educators, learners, parents, and districts;
  clear next actions. Guidance without direct answers is already part of its
  positioning, so ARIA should not claim exclusivity on that basis.
- Synthesis Tutor: https://www.synthesis.com/tutor
  Clear product and audience, prominent trial action, lesson examples, and
  practical questions. Borrow the clarity, not its claims about outcomes,
  adoption counts, pricing, testimonials, or age range.
- Mathspace student workbook:
  https://help.mathspace.co/basic-workbook-features
  Intermediate-step checking and graduated hints are already established
  tutoring features. Describe ARIA's specific implementation without claiming
  to be the first or only system to do this. Its public marketing page was
  unavailable during this review; the official workbook documentation was read.

## Applied structure

1. Name the product and subject immediately: ARIA Math Tutor.
2. Explain the learner experience in familiar language.
3. Show logged mathematical examples: a valid step, an error, and answer-seeking.
4. Offer a teacher preview and a separate school-study planning route.
5. Answer practical questions about scope, availability, and data handling.
6. Keep the research roadmap and limitations accessible in an expandable band
   and on dedicated research pages.

The positioning is a research focus, not a proven competitive advantage:
checking a step and deciding when a small intervention is warranted while
preserving opportunities for independent reasoning. No learning-gain claim,
readiness guarantee, live website tutor, or unverified feature was added.

The website preserves its existing palette, visual identity, routes, and
research sections. It does not copy competitor layouts, branding, or text.
