“For over a century, education has been designed for the average student.”
01 / THE GAP

The Gap

Students with ADHD and learning disabilities represent 20% of all students but 0% of AI tutor design decisions. Standard AI tutors answer questions. That’s not what neurodivergent students need.

02 / THE DEFICIT

The Real Problem

The deficit isn’t content knowledge — it’s metacognition. Planning before starting. Monitoring while working. Catching your own errors. These are learnable skills that ADHD specifically impairs. School never teaches them explicitly.

03 / THE OPPORTUNITY

Why This Matters

Bloom’s 1984 “2 Sigma” study showed one-on-one tutoring outperforms classroom instruction by two standard deviations. AI can scale this — but only if it’s built for how neurodivergent students actually learn.

Method

How ARIA Works

ARIA combines language, behavioral signals, and state-specific prompts to make the learning process—not the final answer—the center of intervention.

Think-Aloud Protocol

Before attempting any problem, students type or speak their reasoning out loud. ARIA listens to how they think — their hesitations, false starts, and moments of confusion — not just what answer they give.

Cognitive State Detection

A natural language classifier trained on 3,507 synthetic think-aloud samples detects seven cognitive states in real time: Planning, Flow, Confused, Rushing, Frustrated, Stuck, and Insight. Accuracy: 80.0%; macro-F1: 0.796.

Metacognitive Intervention

Instead of answering, ARIA asks one targeted question based on the detected state. A student who is rushing gets asked to articulate their plan. A student who is stuck gets the smallest possible question to get them moving again.

Transfer Detection

The core research question: does the student start self-initiating metacognitive behaviors without being prompted? ARIA tracks this longitudinally. Transfer F1: 0.886.

Privacy by Architecture

Everything runs locally on the student’s device using on-device LLM inference. No data ever leaves the machine. Not by policy — by architecture. Designed specifically for schools with FERPA and HIPAA requirements.

Behavioral Fusion

Keystroke timing, typing speed, pause duration, and backspace rate are captured alongside text. Behavioral features are generator-agnostic — they don’t depend on how any language model writes, they depend on how the student types.

Research origin

Why We’re Building This

ARIA began with Naren’s experience of ADHD and a recurring problem with AI tutors: they answered his questions when what he needed was help learning how to think through problems independently. The missing skill was not content knowledge, but metacognition — planning, monitoring, and recognizing when to change course.

Today, Naren Saravanan and Karthick Mallireddy are developing ARIA as an independent research initiative. The system detects cognitive states in real time and deliberately refuses to give students answers, responding instead with the smallest targeted question that can help them move forward.

ARIA is open research because this problem is bigger than one student and one project. We are looking for researchers, educators, and students who want to help determine whether AI can teach neurodivergent students to self-regulate — not just perform.
